# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Gael Uribe (G-Darko) for software/web roles under time pressure — they need identity, proof, and a contact path in seconds.

Secondary: potential freelance/client leads who want a sense of craft and personality before reaching out.

Both audiences matter; recruiter scan-path wins when tradeoffs appear.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that gets Gael contacted or invited to interview. Success means a stranger can understand who he is, trust the work enough to act, and send a message or open a clear channel — not that they complete every HUD module or easter egg.

## Positioning

G-Darko’s portfolio should feel like the same mind that ships his products: curious, systems-oriented, playful with tech, bilingual. The futuristic HUD / OS metaphor is an expression of that personality and of the kinds of interfaces he builds — not a requirement that the site literally behave like an operating system. Work evidence and contact lead; atmosphere and personality support them.

## Operating Context

Visited on desktop and mobile browsers; often as a cold link from a résumé, GitHub, or application. Session may include Spanish or English. Contact goes through an in-site form (Formspree). A printable/readable CV route exists alongside the interactive surface. GitHub (`G-Darko`) is a primary external proof channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router portfolio with interactive HUD shell, missions/projects, skills, certifications, contact form, optional terminal and minigame/easter-egg layers, theme palettes, and ES/EN locale.
- Bilingual ES/EN is required.
- HUD chrome, boot sequence, terminal, easter eggs, and minigames may be demoted, shortened, or removed when they hurt the hire path — the goal is a better portfolio, not preserving every mechanic.
- Open: exact depth of HUD retention after redesign (atmosphere vs full OS desk) is decided per surface work, guided by “work leads, personality remains.”

## Brand Commitments

- Name / mark: Gael Uribe · G-Darko (legal/full name Isaac Gael Uribe Ortiz appears on CV).
- Voice: bilingual; can be technical and playful, but primary paths must stay plain enough for recruiters.
- Personality: futuristic / systems / product-builder energy should still be readable after changes — without forcing jargon or unskippable theater in front of the work.
- Assets in play: profile image (`img/DARKO.png`), mission/media content where shipped, GitHub, email on CV (`gdarko.uribe@gmail.com`).

## Evidence on Hand

- Profile copy and role (Software / Web Developer; IT Engineer framing in terminal/CV).
- Missions / project briefs (including orgs such as Black Sheep Lab and others in content); some entries still use mockup placeholders — do not invent screenshots or case metrics.
- Certifications and skills panels in the HUD.
- CV page at `/cv`.
- Contact form via Formspree; no alternate in-HUD mailto is currently surfaced as a primary control.
- GitHub: https://github.com/G-Darko

## Product Principles

1. **Contact is the win** — every first-viewport and primary path should make reaching Gael easier, not harder.
2. **Proof before costume** — real work and identity lead; HUD personality rides as atmosphere, not as the product.
3. **Personality without friction** — futuristic flavor is welcome when it reveals who he is; it must not block skip, scan, or mobile thumb-reach.
4. **Bilingual by default** — ES and EN stay first-class; no primary path English-only or Spanish-only.
5. **Cut to improve** — mechanics that dilute the hire path can go; keep only what makes the result clearly better than today.
